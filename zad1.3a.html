Classic Pancake Recipe

Perfectly fluffy pancakes, great for breakfast or brunch. Quick and easy to make!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda

Instructions

  1. Mix all the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Whisk together the wet ingredients separately.
  3. Combine the dry and wet ingredients, stirring until smooth.
  4. Cook on a hot griddle until golden brown, flipping once.
